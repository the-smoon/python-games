---
name: Audio-driven attack selection
description: Why song characteristics must outweigh deterministic variation when choosing enemy and boss patterns.
---

Select motion and attack options primarily from the live song features. Use deterministic variation only to break near-ties, not to rotate through a fixed list regardless of the audio.

**Why:** Rotating the winning choice by an entity serial made distinct songs produce nearly the same mix of behaviors, despite apparently responsive scores. Even a modest variation weight could obscure quiet-versus-bright musical differences in regression checks.

**How to apply:** When adding patterns or tuning selection scores, compare contrasting bass-heavy, mid/tempo-heavy, and bright/transient-heavy signals at fixed serials. Keep variation smaller than a meaningful difference between scored audio characteristics.

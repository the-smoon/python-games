---
name: Audio-driven encounter design
description: How pre-match song motifs and live audio share control of encounter appearance and behavior.
---

Analyze the whole selected track before the match and use its section motifs for enemy/boss silhouettes and baseline movement identity. During play, keep live audio active for spawn timing and size, attack and projectile choices, combat intensity, and beat-triggered movement changes. Live changes should adapt behavior without discarding the song's encounter-design bias.

Use deterministic variation only to break near-ties, not to rotate through a fixed list regardless of the audio.

**Why:** The user requested a clear hierarchy: pre-match analysis establishes encounter design, while playback remains responsive to the evolving live soundtrack. Rotating patterns by an entity serial also made distinct songs produce nearly the same mix of behaviors.

**How to apply:** When adjusting encounter profiles, keep the full-track map as the design baseline and compare contrasting bass-heavy and bright songs. Keep live responses distinct, and keep deterministic variation smaller than meaningful audio differences.

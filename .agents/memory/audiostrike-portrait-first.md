---
name: Portrait-first gameplay
description: Why AudioStrike gameplay prioritizes portrait phone visibility and continuous ship visibility.
---

Keep AudioStrike's playable area portrait-first for a Pixel 9-sized phone and never make the ship disappear as an invincibility indicator.

**Why:** The user reported that the landscape-scaled game was too small to see on their Pixel 9 and that the ship appeared missing while it continued taking damage. A flashing hide/show damage effect is especially misleading when the ship is already small.

**How to apply:** When changing playfield dimensions, HUD, boss motion, or damage effects, check a portrait phone viewport with an active round. Use an always-visible shield or color effect for invincibility, and keep movement bounds clear of HUD overlays.
